THE REFLECTION

It took me around five years to become comfortable saying, "I write music."

Calling myself a composer felt very different. It carried a weight I didn't feel I had earned.

And yet the music kept arriving. Composition after composition. Never once asking whether I felt ready for it. No warning. No particular pattern. It simply kept coming.

Eventually I realised, accepting who you are isn't arrogance. Sometimes, it's simply honesty. Today I understand the following:

"To have a gift of bringing something to life that only existed in your mind… is the most intimately delicate part,... of the I AM."
