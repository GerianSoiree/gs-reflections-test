Reflection 4

The Composer I Didn't Believe I Was

For a long time, I did not fully believe that I could call myself a composer. Yet the music continued to arrive.

Music: Younger Today

Younger Today was written for my son when he left home to work abroad. Creating this piece became part of a much deeper realisation for me. I had to come to terms with the fact that I am a composer.

The music I create often exists in my mind before it exists anywhere else. There is something deeply meaningful and almost sacred about being able to bring into existence something that did not exist before.

I know the ability is there. Sometimes the music comes to me daily. At other times, there are periods of complete silence. The process is closely connected to the emotional space and state of mind in which I find myself.

To finally accept that I am a composer was not about claiming a title. It was about recognising the gift and learning to trust what was already there.

The Composer I Didn't Believe I Was.
