Reflection 7

When Life Changes the Music

The people who shape our lives never really leave us. Sometimes, they simply become part of the music we carry within us.

Music: Emma's Garden

This reflection is one of the most emotional parts of my musical journey. My late mother-in-law had a profound influence on my life and on my work.

As I witnessed her deterioration through Alzheimer's disease, I experienced something deeply moving. There was a particular piece of music that I often played for her.

Sometimes she would hear the same piece several times within a week and, because she could no longer remember hearing it before, she would experience it as if it were the first time.

Yet each time, the music moved her deeply.

That experience changed the way I understand music. It reminded me that music can reach places that words sometimes cannot, and that even when memories begin to disappear, emotional connections can remain.

Emma's Garden carries its own story and meaning, one that I will share more fully at another stage of this journey.

The people who shape our lives never really leave us. Sometimes, they simply become part of the music we carry within us.
