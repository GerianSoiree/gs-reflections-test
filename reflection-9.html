THE REFLECTION

Looking back at this journey, I realise that every composition has taught me something.

Not only about music, but about myself. About patience, uncertainty, creativity, and the courage to keep going when I didn't always know where the music was leading.

When I first started composing, I thought I was simply learning how to write music. What I didn't realise was that I was also learning how to listen, not only to the music, but to myself.

Every piece became part of that process. Some were difficult. Some came easily. Some changed completely from what I originally imagined. And each one left something behind.

Perhaps that is what reflection really means. Looking back, not to change what happened, but to understand what it taught us.

When I look at the journey now, I don't see ten separate reflections. I see a collection of moments that gradually became part of who I am.
