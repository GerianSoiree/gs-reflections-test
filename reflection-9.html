THE REFLECTION

People hear the finished composition.
What they don't hear, is the person I became while creating it.

Behind every piece of music is a journey that cannot always be explained through notes, melodies or recordings. There are moments of uncertainty, excitement, and times when I question whether the next composition will ever find what I am searching for.

Some pieces have taught me to wait and stop forcing the music, allowing it to reveal itself in its own time. Others have taught me acceptance, especially when the music becomes something different from what I originally imagined.

Once the music piece was completed, I realised it was never the only thing being created.
I was changing too.

With every composition, something within me has moved, grown or quietly changed. I may begin a piece with a story or emotion, sometimes it evolves into it with a different understanding of myself, life, or the emotions that inspired the music.

I don't compose simply to make music. I compose to discover who I become through making it.

Every finished composition becomes more than a piece of music. It becomes a marker along my own journey. A reflection and a reminder of who I had become when the final note was played.
