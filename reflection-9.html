Reflection 9

Every Composition Changes Me

The music may one day be forgotten, but the person it helped me become will remain, long after the music has stopped playing.

Music: Mothers

Mothers was one of the fastest pieces of music I have ever composed. From the moment I sat down to create it, the music developed quickly, and within approximately two and a half hours, the composition was complete.

The piece was created around Mother's Day and was shared publicly as a musical tribute. It was beautifully received and reminded me once again of how quickly an emotional idea can sometimes become music.

Every composition I create leaves something behind in me. The music may begin as a melody, an emotion or an idea, but by the time the composition is finished, I have travelled somewhere with it.

Creating music changes the way I listen, the way I feel and sometimes even the way I understand myself.

The composition may eventually belong to the listener, and one day it may even be forgotten. But the journey of creating it remains part of me.

The music may one day be forgotten, but the person it helped me become will remain, long after the music has stopped playing.
