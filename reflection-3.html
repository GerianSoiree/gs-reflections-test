THE REFLECTION

For almost thirty years, the piano became a memory. Something I used to do. Life moved on. Career, family, responsibility, life. And somewhere in all of that, music quietly stepped aside. It was not a part of who I was at that time. I didn't stop loving it… I stopped living it.

Then one day, I found my way back. What surprised me wasn't that I could still play. It was a feeling of return. Of expectation. As if the music had been waiting for me.

Almost as if those thirty years hadn't taken anything away at all. I was being prepared to hear it internally. To learn how to listen to the music that came from within.

Looking back now, I don't see thirty years as silence anymore. I see thirty years of life, quietly preparing me for what I was about to say.
