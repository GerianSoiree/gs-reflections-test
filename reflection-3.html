Reflection 3

30 Years of Silence

This reflection is about the years I spent separated from music and the extraordinary experience of finding my way back to it as if I had never been away.

Music: Blue

Blue began as a piano piece originally composed with an electric guitar lead in mind and later developed into a blues-inspired piece. This rendition explores the music through a neoclassical interpretation.

It reflects the years of silence between myself and music, and the moment I returned to it and discovered that the connection was still there, waiting for me.
