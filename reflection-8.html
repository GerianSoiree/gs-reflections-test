THE REFLECTION

For many years, I thought purpose was something you found once. A single moment, a single answer. I don't see it that way anymore.

For much of my life, music was always somewhere inside me. But life has a way of taking us in different directions. There are responsibilities, careers, families and all the things that demand our attention. And sometimes the things that matter deeply to us become quieter.

For me, music eventually found its way back into my life. It was never planned, I never planned to become a writer composer. It simply happened.

I found fulfillment in creating. It made me feel connected to a part of myself that I had perhaps not fully known before. Purpose isn't a destination, It's a relationship. And the emotions it creates deepens as you grow. Perhaps some things need time.

Looking back, I don't think life suddenly changed direction. I think I finally recognised something that had been quietly waiting for me.
