Reflection 8

Finding Purpose Later

Perhaps purpose isn't waiting somewhere in the future. Perhaps it's been quietly walking beside us all along.

Music: Questions

Questions is deeply connected to curiosity and the idea that discovering ourselves often begins by being willing to ask questions.

The music moves through changing emotions, unusual chord progressions and moments of mystery. Different musical ideas appear and develop, creating emotional peaks along the journey.

For me, finding purpose later in life has meant understanding that perhaps I wasn't actually searching for something completely new. Perhaps some of the answers were already developing quietly beside me.

Creativity, music and self-discovery have all become part of that journey. The more questions I asked, the more I discovered about myself.

Questions reminds me that curiosity is not a weakness. Sometimes asking the question is the beginning of finding the answer.

Perhaps purpose isn't waiting somewhere in the future. Perhaps it's been quietly walking beside us all along.
