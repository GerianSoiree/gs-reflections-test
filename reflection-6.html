THE REFLECTION

For a long time, I questioned whether I had the right to call myself a composer.

I compared myself to trained pianists. Accomplished musicians, people with years of formal education behind them.

Then one day I realised something. I wasn't competing with their journey. This journey of discovery was my own.

Imposter syndrome didn't disappear, it simply became quieter.

Perhaps confidence isn't the absence of doubt. Perhaps it's choosing to continue, even when doubt is still sitting quietly beside you.
