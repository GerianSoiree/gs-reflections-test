Reflection 5

AI Is Not My Creativity

AI is a tool. It is not the source of my creativity.

Music: Seeds

Seeds is a reflection about inheritance, legacy and the things we leave behind. What are we planting during our lives, and how might those seeds continue to grow long after we are gone?

Every person leaves something behind. Through our actions, our words, our creativity and the lives we touch, we plant seeds that may eventually grow into something greater than ourselves.

My music is deeply emotional. I compose with melodies and sounds that often create an emotional response, a memory, a feeling or an imagined image in the listener's mind.

Just as a carpenter uses different tools to shape and create something, I use different tools in my own creative process. But the tools are not the creator. The creativity, the imagination and the music begin within me.

AI may be one of the tools I use, but AI is not my creativity.

What seeds are you planting for those who will come after you?
