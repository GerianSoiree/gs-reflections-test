THE REFLECTION

People often ask whether AI creates my music.

My answer is simple. No. Never.

The music begins long before I touch a piano, or open a computer. It begins the same place it always has. AI doesn't replace that. It helps me translate what I already hear into something you can experience. For me, it's no different to a piano, a paintbrush, or a camera. It's an instrument. Nothing more, nothing less.

The real question was never whether a tool can create art. The question is whether the person using that tool has something meaningful to say. Nowadays we hear the term "intellectual property," but it can be seen in many different ways, mostly as a way to protect the creator, whether that creator is man or machine.

From music producers, art collectors, and the dancers on a stage, we all use tools to bring the arts to the world. AI now helps us do this instantaneously. But are we ready?

Creativity doesn't live in the tool. It lives in the person who chooses how to use it.
