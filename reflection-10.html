THE REFLECTION

Some of my clearest thoughts have never arrived in silence. They arrived while saying them out loud. I've realised that reflection isn't about finding answers. It's about giving your thoughts enough space to reveal themselves.

Some conversations are worth having, even when the person asking the questions is yourself.

Reflections are the quiet moments we have when we look inward, rather than ask questions.

This is our moment of self realization.

Let yourself experience those quiet moments in the dark. Those blissful moments in the sun. Those sad moments of grief. The moment you see a child take their first steps in life. To witness someone else's success, in real time.
