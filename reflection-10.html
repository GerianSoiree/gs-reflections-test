Learning Through Reflection

Reflection doesn't always change the question. Sometimes, it changes the person asking it.

Music: Tomorrow

Tomorrow holds a very special place in my musical journey. It was one of the very first pieces of music I composed on my Roland FP-10 electronic piano.

What began as a few notes played almost randomly slowly evolved into a complete composition. The music developed naturally and became one of those moments that reminded me how unexpected creativity can be.

Tomorrow is cinematic in character, reflecting the musical direction I often find myself drawn toward. My music frequently moves through neoclassical and cinematic influences, while also allowing me to experiment with different instruments and sounds.

Sometimes a composition needs the voice of a cello. Sometimes brass, electric guitar, a duduk, an oud, or another unexpected instrument becomes part of the journey.

I enjoy allowing my music to evolve into different forms and genres. It is part of my creative process and part of the freedom I have as a composer.

Tomorrow reminds me that reflection is not always about finding a different answer. Sometimes the real change happens within the person who is willing to keep asking.

Reflection doesn't always change the question. Sometimes, it changes the person asking it.
