THE REFLECTION

There's a question I've carried for years.
Where does the music come from?

For me, it starts quietly. Almost like remembering something, the way you remember a face, a task, or a feeling from long ago suddenly returns to you, uninvited.

At first it's faint. A background tune or melody, barely there. Then, slowly, it gets louder. More defined. Until I can't ignore it any longer. That's when I sit at the piano, and try to follow it home.

Some of what arrives becomes a piece entirely its own. Some find their way into something I wrote years before. There are no rules to it. Only listening.

I've spent years wondering where this ability to hear music comes from. Sometimes I think we're not the source of it at all. Perhaps we're simply able to receive it, the way an old radio finds a frequency that was already playing, long before we tuned in.

I don't say that to sound mystical, but it's the closest I've come to an honest answer.
