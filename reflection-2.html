THE REFLECTION

Now that I’m in my fifties, I’ve come to understand something.
Life sometimes hides new pathways. Not a crossroads, nothing that dramatic. More like a moment you were quietly expecting, even if you couldn’t have named it.

You reach a point where you’re settled. A career, responsibilities, family, friends, and the small worries that come with all of it. And then, without warning, you sense that something bigger is still on its way. I never expected it to happen. But I think, quietly, without knowing it, I’d been shaping it all along.

Perhaps it was never about becoming someone else. Perhaps it’s only ever been about becoming comfortable with the person you were always meant to be.
